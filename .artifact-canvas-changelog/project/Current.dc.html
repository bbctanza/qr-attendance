Attendance & History Rework
Version 2.7.0 · November 6, 2026

Attendance Is Actually Live - The live session said "Live Updates" behind a pulsing dot but only ever loaded its numbers once, so the checked-in count stayed frozen at whatever it was when you opened the page. It now updates as people are scanned.

Fixed: Recent Scans Showed Other Events - The live scan list showed the newest scans from every event, so a session could list check-ins that belonged to a different one.

Fixed: Attendance Rate Could Exceed 100% - The percentage counted everyone who attended, but compared them only against events that recorded absences.

History Opens Quickly - It used to load every event ever recorded, with two lookups each, and then throw away everything outside the month you were viewing.

Member Names No Longer Sent to an Outside Service - Attendance lists built profile pictures by sending names to a third-party image site.
